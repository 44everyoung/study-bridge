# Career explorer sources

Reviewed: October 22, 2026.

The 15 pathway matches are editorial, interest-based associations. They are not admission prerequisites, aptitude scores, program rankings, or guarantees of admission/employment. A listed subject can connect to more than one pathway. Careers may require further training or professional credentials. College study areas include certificates/diplomas and transfer routes, not only university majors.

The 53 choices group BC subjects and course families in plain language, omitting grade numbers to keep the selector readable. This is not an exhaustive course catalogue. Psychology is explicitly labelled as a school elective; it is not presented as a province-wide prescribed course. Course availability varies by school.

## Implementation guidance

- Show a short explanation: “Ideas based on your interests. Check each school’s entry requirements.”
- Label the second group “Majors & programs”, since trades are often certificates or apprenticeships.
- Display an empty university group for skilled trades honestly; do not manufacture a university route.
- A generic college directory is labelled “Explore programs”, not as a verified offer of every major on the card.
- Ivey HBA is not presented as a direct-entry four-year BCom; its official site explains the route.
- No institution is ranked or endorsed above another.
